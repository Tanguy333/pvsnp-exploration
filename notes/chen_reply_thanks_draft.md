**Subject:** Re: Non-symmetric PTE searches — thank you

Dear Professor Chen,

Thank you very much for these precise references — they are exactly the
authoritative bounds I needed. Your exhaustive searches (size 8 up to 570,
size 9 to 592, size 10 to 530, size 11 to 1600, size 12 to 500) settle
several questions I had been considering, and I will defer to them: in
particular I now see that the compact non-symmetric size-8 solution I had
found is already in your tables (p. 229), and that no non-symmetric ideal
exists for sizes 9–12 in these ranges.

My own work is on a slightly different object — the shortest vectors of an
integer lattice associated with these problems (including non-ideal,
"defective" vectors), bounded by support length rather than height — so it
is complementary rather than competing, and your results sharpen
considerably what part of it is genuinely new.

I won't take more of your time now. If the lattice side yields something
that bears on the existence questions you study, I will write again with
concrete results. Thank you again for your generosity with the references.

With best regards and great respect for your work,
Tanguy Le Mer

## The three readings of the matrix

### 1. A single line crosses the 4 columns: line 7 (Williams)

And its crossing of relativization + algebrization is *demonstrated*
(Aaronson's oracle), not merely argued. The structural reason, quoted
from the text ([A-001](../bib/williams_2011.md)): *« improvements over
exhaustive search necessarily exploit structure in instances that black-box
methods cannot see »*. The cost: the bound does not go below NEXP, and
the extension to TC⁰ requires an algorithmic ingredient that nobody has
(quantified empirically by F-001: the Beigel-Tarui-style representation
does not exist for TC⁰ d=2, with IP as witness).

### 2. The classical barriers are no longer the front

The living programmes (lines 7-10) have all, by construction, settled their
relationship with the 3 classical barriers. What blocks them is **each time
an internal barrier of their own**: composite moduli (line 4), missing SAT
algorithm (line 7), BIP 2019 (line 8), lack of a CPT technique (line 9),
locality (line 10). The meta-lesson: *any strategy precise enough to be
executable gives rise to the analysis of its own impossibility*. Asking
"do you cross the 3 barriers?" is necessary but very far from
sufficient.

### 3. The repository's failure patterns are all in the matrix

The repository's 25 rounds classify without remainder: Rounds 1-2 (lines 2-3),
Round 3/6/7 (line 12), Rounds 6-10 drift (outside the matrix — not an approach
to P vs NP), Rounds 12-22 (line 6), Round 23 + F-001 (line 11), Round 24
(line 5), Round 25 (line 9). No round worked on lines 7, 8, 10
*with a technical contribution* — these are the only lines where the
real locks are identified open mathematical problems rather
than demonstrated impossibilities.

**2026-06 update (V thread, outside the rounds)**: line 14 is the first
— and only — cell of the matrix whose lock was **closed by the
repository itself** (refutation of KV's Conjecture 5, validated by an
author), rather than documented from the literature. Observed cost: ~55
V tasks, including a long drift into number theory (V-040→V-050, recorded);
net gain for P vs NP: one closed cell with an executable witness, and the
25th-26th self-corrections as a method (adversarial check before dissemination).

- **E-2 — Post's programme in complexity: autoreducibility and
  mitoticity of complete sets** (candidature no. 6, examination
  PR-009 of 2026-09-08; file
  [tours/candidature_post_autoreductibilite.md](../tours/candidature_post_autoreductibilite.md);
  verbatims [data/pr009_lecture_bfvmt.md](../data/pr009_lecture_bfvmt.md)).
  *Mechanism* (Buhrman-Fortnow-van Melkebeek-Torenvliet, SICOMP 2000;
  arXiv math/9807185 read): positive engine = local checkability of
  computations (Thm 4.1: every ≤ᵖ_T-complete set for EXP is autoreducible;
  Thm 4.3: levels of the exponential hierarchy up to O(log n)
  alternations — « *Theorem 4.3 goes through for k(n) ∈ O(log n)* »);

  ≤ᵖ_{2-T}-complete set for EEXPSPACE that is not autoreducible); a theorem "in

  complete sets of EEXP / EXPSPACE / PSPACE / EXP separates P/PSPACE, NL/NP,

  polynomial reduction and the exponential computation — only separates
  resource classes of different natures, and P vs NP opposes two
  classes of the same resource. *Lock*: method ceiling of the
  positive engine at O(log n) alternations (« *failed to account for the growth of
